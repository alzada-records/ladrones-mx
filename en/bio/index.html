Bio

Ladrones is a band from Guadalajara, Jalisco, Mexico, that blends rock/metal, hip hop and Regional Mexican into a style of their own they call “Flow Pesado.” Since 2021, their music has been breaking down barriers and celebrating the musical diversity of a new generation: free, inclusive and without prejudice.

Three albums

With Flow Pesado, Mexican Pesado and their latest, Arriba La L, Ladrones has quickly earned its place in the scene.
